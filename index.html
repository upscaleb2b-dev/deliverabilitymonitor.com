How it works

  1. 01

    Connect

    Link Google, Microsoft and SMTP inboxes, your sending domains and your sequencer. Read-only, OAuth where it exists.

  2. 02

    Monitor

    Continuous placement tests, auth checks, blacklist sweeps and Postmaster pulls across every asset you connected.

  3. 03

    Diagnose

    AI separates real degradation from daily noise, then names the cause — not just the symptom.

  4. 04

    Act

    Alerts land where you work: Slack, webhook, your agent. Pause the mailbox, rotate the domain, fix the record.
